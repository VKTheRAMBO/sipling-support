Sipling privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · WorldLamp Apps · worldlampapps@gmail.com

What the app collects: nothing

Sipling has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your data. The app does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your watch

  • Your drink log, daily goal, weight, waking hours, cup size, chosen buddy and reminder settings are stored on your Apple Watch only, in the app’s private storage. They are included in your normal watch backup to your iPhone or iCloud, which Apple encrypts.
  • Your Sipling Pro status is checked with Apple’s App Store on the device. Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple; we receive no payment or identity details.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Sipling writes the water you log to Apple Health and reads your exercise minutes to adjust today’s goal. Health data is used only on the device for those two purposes, is never sent anywhere, and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health › Apps.

Notifications and Siri

Reminders are scheduled locally on your watch. Siri requests to log water are processed on the device through Apple’s App Intents; we receive nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the policy changes. Questions: worldlampapps@gmail.com.
